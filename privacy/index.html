Language preference

When you actively select EN or DE, this website stores that choice in your browser’s local storage under tony-willert-portfolio-language. The value is only en or de. It is read to display your preferred language and is not used to create an advertising profile.

The choice stays in that browser until you change it or clear this website’s stored data. Storage and access serve the language feature you request, under Section 25(2)(2) TDDDG. To the extent personal data are involved, the basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, for a consistent language display.

Email enquiries

If you contact me by email, I process the sender address, message content and any information you supply to handle the enquiry. WEB.DE, my email service, is involved in delivery and storage of this correspondence.

For enquiries concerning a possible contract, the basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Other correspondence is processed under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, based on my legitimate interest in responding. I keep correspondence as long as it is needed for that purpose and any applicable statutory retention obligations.

External links and linked applications

LinkedIn, Junico, freelancermap, GitHub and the LearnerCue demo are linked as external destinations. Their pages and privacy terms apply when you open them. No social-media widgets or external booking forms are embedded in this portfolio.

Analytics, fonts and media

The portfolio code does not use advertising trackers or analytics tools. Fonts use your device’s installed system fonts. The images and demonstration video on these pages are delivered through GitHub Pages. There is no contact form; the email link opens your email application.

Your rights

Subject to the conditions in the GDPR, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability. You may object under Article 21 GDPR to processing based on legitimate interests, for reasons arising from your particular situation.

You may lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, particularly in the country where you live or work or where you believe an infringement occurred. For this website, the Brandenburg data protection authority is a relevant contact. To exercise rights against me, use the contact details above.

Scope and changes

These details cover the functions of this portfolio. The separate external demo and freelance platforms have their own processing arrangements. This notice will be updated when the website’s functions or processing change.
